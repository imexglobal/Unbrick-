Unbrick support

Unbrick: Learn Korean is published by IMEX Global. Email hellopawfend@gmail.com and include your iOS version and what you were doing. There is no account to sign in to, and there is no in-app purchase to refund.

Using the app

  • Lessons, cards, and the calendar work without an account. Progress stays on the iPhone.
  • The AI tutor is on the Home screen, labeled “AI tutor”. You can type or speak a Korean answer. The first time, iOS asks for the microphone and for speech recognition.
  • If Apple Intelligence is on, the phone can grade on the device. A build that includes the Unbrick server sends the answer text there, then to Anthropic, and shows “AI tutor is resting, try again tomorrow” when the shared daily budget or the per-device cap is used up.
  • Practice tiles do not close other apps. Screen Time limits are optional and are set in the Settings app. This build does not request Family Controls.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes the microphone, speech, tutor text, the install id used for the daily cap, and local storage. To remove everything the app stored, delete Unbrick. There is no separate account to delete.
